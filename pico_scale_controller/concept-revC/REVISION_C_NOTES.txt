COFFEE SCALE - IMPLEMENTED REVISION C - 2026-11-04

APPROVED CONCEPT
100 x 60 x 1.6mm PCB with R5 corners and four 3.2mm mounting holes.
Pico W rotated vertically, antenna at the upper edge and its unused USB below.
Battery connector retained; load-cell pads/HX711 at the left edge.
OLED above the PCB; two top buttons. USB-C, master power and scroll/select on
the right edge. Charger/protection/regulation grouped on the right; debug
header beside Pico. Bottom-side buzzer. Black solder mask, white silkscreen,
ENIG finish; restrained silkscreen curves do not add decorative copper.

ENCODER AND HARDWARE WAKE
SW1: Bourns PEC12R-2217F-S0024, incremental quadrature encoder with momentary
push. Footprint: RotaryEncoder_Bourns_Horizontal_PEC12R-2x17F-Sxxxx.
Electrical pins A/B/C are encoder channels/common; S1/S2 are push contacts.
Mechanical SH anchors are tied to protected GND. The stock footprint has
generous plated anchor slots; check physical seating before final assembly.
Manufacturer drawing: https://www.bourns.com/docs/product-datasheets/pec12r.pdf

The encoder press grounds WAKE_N. Q5 (AO3401A) turns on from RAW_SW and feeds
HOLD_EN through D3. R18 pulls Q5 gate to its source when the button is released.
D2 allows Pico PWR_HOLD to sustain HOLD_EN independently. D2/D3 isolate the
two drive paths. R16=100k discharges HOLD_EN; C20=10uF gives a nominal ~1s RC
time constant, with a shorter effective startup/off interval due to capacitor
DC-bias derating and the switch threshold. Measure the usable startup interval.
Q6 (AO3400A) isolates the 3.3V GPIO from WAKE_N/RAW_SW: ENC_PUSH is ACTIVE HIGH
while pressed, LOW when released. No battery-voltage divider drives a GPIO.
The A/B channels have 10k pull-ups and 10nF capacitors; use proper quadrature
state decoding and software debounce. C19 filters the press input.

FIRMWARE INTERFACE
GP0/GP1 UART TX/RX; GP4/GP5 OLED SDA/SCL; GP6/GP7 HX711 DOUT/SCK.
GP8: BTN1 / TARE, active low. GP9: BTN2 / START-STOP, active low.
GP10: ENC_A, active low contact. GP11: ENC_B, active low contact.
GP12: buzzer PWM. GP13: PWR_HOLD. GP14: ENC_PUSH, active high.
Assert GP13 immediately at boot, before display/WiFi startup or file operations.
Scroll changes the menu; press selects and also provides hardware wake.
For firmware shutdown, finish saving data and wait for the press to be released,
then drive GP13 LOW and wait for power to fall. A held press keeps Q5 on.
The inactivity timeout remains a firmware setting; the planned default is one
hour. Firmware has not been supplied or tested in this hardware revision.

MECHANICAL FILES / LIMITATIONS
coffee-scale-revC.step includes the PCB and parts, a photo-based OLED reference,
an encoder clearance envelope, and a separate proposed edge wheel.
models/Thumbwheel_D14_prototype.stl is oriented for printing, rim face down.
Wheel: 14mm nominal diameter, 6mm total axial depth, D bore with 6.2mm circular
diameter and 4.7mm flat-to-opposite-wall dimension. It is a fit prototype;
printer tolerances, shaft engagement, 0.5mm press travel and retainer/clearance
must be checked physically. Support the encoder on the fixed case structure.
The encoder body model is locally generated and conservative, not a supplier
production model: assumed axis height 10mm, body envelope top 16.7mm above
PCB top. Start with at least 17.5mm top clearance plus enclosure tolerances.
The actual selected encoder and wheel may set the case height, not the OLED.
The side wheel extends about 3.25mm beyond the right PCB edge.

OLED photo dimensions: 27.38 x 27.72mm PCB, 21.24 x 21.70mm mounting-hole spacing.
PCB underside is provisionally 10mm above main PCB top; modelled total height
12.5mm. Hole diameter 2.2mm, thickness 1.2mm, header offsets and glass dimensions
remain assumptions. Measure the real module before printing the enclosure.
Its four supports attach to the case; no OLED carrier mounting holes were added.
The Pico model shows direct mounting. Add the actual socket height if socketed.
Allow at least 4mm below PCB for the buzzer; 6mm standoffs are a starting point.
The two top buttons need guided case plungers; provide free travel and no preload.
Keep metal screws, battery foil and wiring out of the Pico antenna region.

COFFEE-SCALE STRUCTURE
Mount one load-cell end to the rigid base and the other to the weighing platform
according to its actual drawing. PCB, encoder and button supports belong to the
fixed base. Avoid rubbing, cable tension and any bridge to the moving platform.
Add overload stops appropriate to the rated cell deflection. No enclosure or
load-cell mounting assembly is included in this electronics export.

UNCHANGED POWER / PROTOTYPE REVIEW
USB-C is charge/power only. Charging nominally 100mA using MCP73831; load sharing,
DW01A protection and buck-boost 3.3V retained. No cell thermistor is implemented.
JST PH S2B-PH-K-S is 2.00mm pitch: pin1 BAT_P, pin2 raw BATT_NEG.
Do not short raw BATT_NEG to protected GND. Verify the real cell specification,
polarity, charging/discharging limits and temperature limits before powering.
Pico pin36 receives external 3.3V; pin37 disables its onboard regulator. Do not
connect the Pico USB. Socket mounting needs SWD jumpers to its three debug pads.
Master OFF disconnects system power while USB charging/protection remain active.
This is unbuilt hardware. Measure startup, shutdown current, charging behaviour
and weighing noise with WiFi/buzzer/USB active before treating it as validated.
