PRESERVED ANCHORS

Footprint origins relative to the board are in enclosure-coordinates.json.

PLACEMENT AND ROUTING
Moved supporting parts away from the owner's button and connector positions.
Regulator, inductor and bypass/output capacitors form a compact lower cell.
ADC filters remain near the upper HX711; charger/protection remain near USB/BAT.
Aligned all 20 capacitors and 18 resistors on horizontal physical axes; 0/180
degree electrical orientations keep local connections short. Thirteen functional
groups retain intentional row spacing rather than scattering individual parts.
Refilled top/bottom protected-ground pours and stitched separate copper areas.
The narrow load-switch ground escape uses one 0.55mm via with 0.30mm drill;
other vias are 0.60/0.30mm. Copper clearances remain 0.20mm minimum.

Assembly alignment helps repeatability, inspection and programming. Each part
still needs placement; actual machine time depends on feeder layout, nozzle
changes, travel optimization and the assembler's program. Buzzer remains on
the bottom, so SMD population still involves both sides.
assembly-reference.csv is a generic KiCad SMD-only reference, excluding parts
with through-hole pads and DNP parts. Coordinates use KiCad absolute origin,
millimetres and Y-up; rotations are native KiCad conventions. It is not an
assembler-approved CPL; feeder/nozzle/rotation verification is still required.
No assembly service or order was submitted.
