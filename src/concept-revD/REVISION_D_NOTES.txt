COFFEE SCALE - REVISION D - 2026-11-04

REQUESTED PLACEMENT
Battery JST PH connector moved beside the USB-C/charging area, opening toward
the upper edge. Vertical Pico W moved left with SWD/UART header beside it.
HX711, input support parts and four load-cell solder pads occupy the upper
portion. OLED shifted down/left to clear the analogue block. Two top buttons,
right-side master power and bottom-side buzzer retained. Board remains
100 x 60 x 1.6mm, R5 corners, four 3.2mm NPTH mounting holes, black mask/white
silkscreen with ENIG finish.

SPECIFIED MENU CONTROL
SW1: Same Sky TS43-135255-10-BK-200-SMT-TR (TR denotes packaging).
This is a momentary directional lever with centre push, not a free-spinning
quadrature encoder. Lever travel is +/-20 degrees; contacts have two stages.
Pin 1 is the first CCW contact, pin 5 the first CW contact; both are used.
Pin 3 is centre push. C is common/protected GND. Pins 2 and 4 are second-stage
direction contacts and intentionally unconnected. Shell lands are grounded.
The previous Bourns encoder and printed wheel are not used in this revision.

FOOTPRINT / CAD
Local Scale:TS43_135255 follows the manufacturer drawing: six 0.7 x 1.7mm
signal lands on 1.5mm pitch, 1.1 x 2.0mm outer shell lands, 0.7mm square
inner shell lands and two locating holes. Prototype locating holes are 0.9mm
instead of the nominal 0.7mm drawing callout, to give assembly allowance for
the manufacturer's metal-tab CAD envelope. Hole centres are +/-4.7,-4.7mm
relative to the footprint. Check seating of the physical part before assembly.
The aligned manufacturer STEP tab vertex envelope radius is 0.382mm within
each 0.45mm hole radius. This checks CAD alignment, not part tolerance/fit.
The manufacturer model places the body about 2.7mm above the main PCB top.
It is substantially lower than the previous encoder/printed-wheel assembly.
The photo-based OLED stack (12.5mm provisional) again determines nominal top
clearance; start with at least 13mm plus actual socket and enclosure tolerances.
The manufacturer model was rotated -90 degrees around its original Z axis,
then translated (0,3.6,0.45)mm to align its leads and body to the PCB pattern.

GPIO / WAKE
GP0/GP1 UART TX/RX; GP4/GP5 OLED SDA/SCL; GP6/GP7 HX711 DOUT/SCK.
GP8: BTN1/TARE active low. GP9: BTN2/START-STOP active low.
GP10: NAV_UP/CCW contact active low. GP11: NAV_DOWN/CW contact active low.
GP12: buzzer PWM. GP13: PWR_HOLD. GP14: NAV_PUSH active HIGH.
Use independent debounced direction switches and repeat while held, not a
quadrature decoder. Centre press selects and also wakes the hardware latch.
Q5/D3 retain the RAW_SW wake path; Q6 isolates the GPIO from battery voltage.
R6/R7 pull up the directions and C17/C18 filter them. C19 filters the push.
Assert GP13 immediately on boot, before WiFi/display/storage initialization.
R16/C20 give a nominal ~1s RC constant; capacitor derating and switch threshold
reduce usable startup time. Bench-check boot margin. Before firmware shutdown,
finish saving, wait for centre press release, drive GP13 low and wait for power
to fall. Holding centre mechanically keeps the wake path active.
One-hour inactivity remains a firmware setting; firmware is not supplied here.

CASE / PROTOTYPE REVIEW
coffee-scale-revD.step is the current electronics reference; use the coordinate
JSON and actual physical parts for case openings and travel clearances.
The OLED model uses the user's 27.38 x 27.72mm outline and 21.24 x 21.70mm
mounting-hole spacing. Its thickness, 2.2mm hole diameter, 10mm underside height
and glass/header offsets remain provisional. Case supports carry its four holes.
The Pico model is direct mounted; add the selected socket height if removable.
Allow at least 4mm below the main PCB for the buzzer; 6mm standoffs are a starting
point. Provide guided top-button plungers with free travel and no preload.
Support the TS43 on the fixed enclosure; provide room for its lever and press.
Keep the weighing platform mechanically independent of PCB/control supports.
Follow the actual load-cell mounting drawing, relieve its wires and add overload
stops. Do not bridge the moving platform to the base through cables or case parts.

POWER UNCHANGED
USB-C is charge/power only. 100mA nominal MCP73831 charging, DW01A cell protection,
load sharing and external 3.3V buck-boost retained. No cell thermistor is fitted.
Verify the actual LP503040 cell limits and JST polarity before powering.
JST pin1=BAT_P, pin2=raw BATT_NEG. Never short raw BATT_NEG to protected GND.
Pico pin36 is supplied externally; pin37 disables its regulator. Do not connect
Pico USB. Debug 3V3 is reference only; socketed Pico needs three SWD jumpers.
Master OFF shuts the load off while charging/protection remain active.
This is unbuilt hardware: measure charging, shutdown current, wake margin and
load-cell noise with WiFi/buzzer/USB active before treating it as validated.
