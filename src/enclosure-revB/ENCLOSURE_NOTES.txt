COFFEE SCALE - REV B ENCLOSURE REFERENCE - 2026-11-04

This revision is the current primary PCB. Rev A fabrication files are retained
for history and do not describe this revised placement. Use fabrication/revB.
The board is an unbuilt prototype; clean CAD checks do not establish weighing
accuracy, EMC, battery qualification or enclosure fit.

PLACEMENT
- Original 100 x 50mm, R5 outline and four 3.2mm mounting holes retained.
- Battery JST PH horizontal connector retained in its original location.
- Load-cell solder pads moved to the top edge near the battery side.
- HX711 and input filter/decoupling parts moved into the top analogue region.
- USB-C opening faces the right edge; opening centre is 22mm below the top.
- Four main buttons remain top operated, at X=84mm, Y=13/21/29/37mm.
  These are BTN1 / BTN3 / BTN4 / BTN2 respectively; firmware may map functions.
- WAKE moved to X=56mm, Y=44.5mm, clear of the display actuator opening.
- Buzzer and two support resistors (R3, R15) are on the bottom face.
- Both ground pours are electrically connected. Analogue performance still
  requires an assembled-board noise/zero-drift test with WiFi and buzzer active.

OLED MODEL - PROVISIONAL HEIGHT APPROVED BY USER
Photo-based PCB size: 27.38 x 27.72mm.
Photo-based mounting-hole centre spacing: 21.24 x 21.70mm.
Four 2.54mm header pins match J3: GND, 3V3, SCL, SDA.
Assumed module PCB thickness 1.2mm; assumed hole diameter 2.2mm.
Assumed module PCB underside 10mm above the main PCB top surface.
Assumed glass/window adds 1.3mm above the module PCB top; total 12.5mm
above the main PCB top surface. Header/edge offsets and glass/active window
dimensions are illustrative; verify with the physical display before printing.
The model is a locally generated clearance reference, not a manufacturer model.
Use all four module holes for case supports; do not rely on the header alone.
The main PCB has no additional OLED mounting holes; the case supplies supports.

3D / COORDINATES
coffee-scale-enclosure-reference.step includes the PCB, installed library
models, OLED reference, square 12 x 12 x 3mm buzzer envelope and power-switch
body/travel envelope. All included STEP shapes were checked for validity.
The OLED pin centres were checked against the four J3 pin coordinates.
enclosure-coordinates.json and enclosure-layout.svg use a top view with origin
at the PCB upper-left outline corner; X goes right, Y goes down, units mm.
The STEP uses the same X origin; its Y is negative down the board and Z is up.
STEP PCB bottom is Z=0; nominal board thickness is 1.6mm. KiCad splits substrate,
mask and conductor thickness, so its substrate solid alone measures 1.51mm.
The stock Pico model represents direct mounting. Socket bodies/heights are not
modelled; add the chosen socket stack height when checking a removable Pico.

CASE STARTING POINT
Allow at least 4mm clear space below the PCB for the 3mm nominal buzzer body,
including its stated dimensional tolerance and solder height; 6mm standoffs
are a useful starting assumption. Keep a sound opening below the buzzer.
Allow at least 13mm above the PCB top for this provisional display stack.
Main buttons and WAKE need guided case plungers because the lid is above their
stock actuators. Size plungers from the chosen switch drawing and actual travel,
with free clearance and no preload. Keep the main button caps clear of the OLED
module edge; their centres are only 2.5mm beyond its right-hand PCB edge.
The master switch faces the lower edge. Its travel envelope ends 1.7mm inside
the PCB outline, so provide a recessed access pocket or a guided slider extension.
Include USB plug overmould clearance and strain relief, not only the metal socket.
Keep screw heads/washers inside the provided mounting-hole clearance envelopes.

COFFEE-SCALE MECHANICS
Fix one end of the load cell to the rigid case base and the other to the weighing
platform, following the actual cell's mounting drawing. PCB supports and display/
button supports belong to the fixed structure. No case wall, cable, screw or
plunger may bridge the moving platform to the base or rub the platform.
Add overload stops with clearance appropriate to the cell's rated deflection.
Route and strain-relieve the four load-cell wires on the fixed side; do not let
wire tension bias the measurement. Keep analogue wiring away from USB/power paths.
Reserve battery space from its real dimensions, with no foil or metal near the
Pico antenna. Battery polarity and the actual cell charging limits remain to be
confirmed; the 100mA charge setting and protection circuit are unchanged.
Place spill barriers/drainage away from USB, the buzzer opening and electronics.
No water-ingress rating has been established.
