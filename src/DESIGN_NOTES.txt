PICO SCALE CONTROLLER - ROUTED REVISION E / OWNER PLACEMENT

SCHEMATIC / BOARD
The schematic and PCB use matching named nets. All connections are routed on
two copper layers, with filled ground pours and ground stitching vias.
Final KiCad checks: zero ERC violations, zero DRC violations, zero unconnected
items and zero schematic/PCB parity issues. All 288 named pads were checked
against the exported schematic netlist.
The board has 68 footprints, 501 track segments and 165 vias. Signal tracks
are 0.20mm; wider power tracks are 0.40mm. Outline: 100x60mm, R5 corners.
These are prototype fabrication outputs, not electrically tested hardware.
Verify the battery specification, connector polarity and enclosure/display fit
before ordering/assembly. See fabrication/revE/README.txt for ordering settings.
See concept-revD/REVISION_D_NOTES.txt for the TS43/wake circuit, manufacturer
control model, OLED reference, case coordinates and firmware interface.
Battery connector is beside USB; Pico/debug are left; HX711/pads are upper.
Rev E preserves all 16 owner/mechanical anchors from the saved November 4
layout. Supporting circuitry was repositioned and rerouted; all 38 R/C parts
have horizontal physical axes, grouped by function. Local decoupling and the
switching cell remain compact. See revision-revE/REVISION_E_NOTES.txt.
Rev A/B/C/D exports are historical; the main PCB matches revision-revE.
The exact owner layout is backed up in backup-user-layout-before-revE.
Original files are in backup-before-corrections-2225. The pre-routing board is
also preserved in backup-before-routing.

PICO PIN ASSIGNMENT
GP0 UART_TX; GP1 UART_RX; GP4 I2C_SDA; GP5 I2C_SCL.
GP6 HX_DOUT; GP7 HX_SCK; GP8 BTN1/TARE; GP9 BTN2/START-STOP.
GP10 NAV_UP; GP11 NAV_DOWN; GP12 BUZZ_PWM; GP13 PWR_HOLD; GP14 NAV_PUSH.
SW2/SW3 and TS43 direction contacts are active low with external 10k pull-ups.
NAV_PUSH is active HIGH through Q6, isolated from RAW_SW/battery voltage.
TS43 uses momentary directions and centre press, not quadrature decoding.

POWER
The carrier supplies regulated 3.3V directly to Pico pin 36.
Pico pin 37 (3V3_EN) is grounded to disable its onboard switching regulator.
Pico VSYS and VBUS are intentionally not used. Do not connect its USB port.
The local Pico symbol reflects this external-power mode in its ERC pin types.

USB-C is charging/power only, with separate 5.1k CC pull-down resistors.
MCP73831T-2ACI/OT is the 4.2V charger; R1=10k sets nominal 100mA charging.
Schottky diode and P-channel MOSFET provide USB/battery load sharing.
DW01A and two opposing N-channel MOSFETs provide cell protection.
BAT_P is cell positive; BATT_NEG is RAW cell negative; GND is protected return.
Do not short BATT_NEG to GND or bypass the protection MOSFETs.

SW6 disconnects the system load. Charging and battery protection stay active.
Press SW1 TS43 centre to wake; firmware must assert GP13 HIGH immediately.
Q5/D3 provide hardware wake. Q6 translates the press into an isolated GPIO.
C20 provides a nominal ~1s HOLD_EN RC constant; bench-check usable startup time.
Wait for centre press release before dropping PWR_HOLD for shutdown.
Firmware must assert GP13 early, before Wi-Fi initialization. After one hour
without button activity, meaningful load-cell change or user Wi-Fi activity,
firmware releases GP13 LOW to remove system power. Background Wi-Fi traffic
should not by itself reset the inactivity timer. Firmware is not included here.

BATTERY VERIFICATION STILL REQUIRED
LP503040 is a size designation, not a complete manufacturer specification.
Verify 4.2V chemistry, charge/discharge limits, low-voltage limits, polarity,
connector wiring and allowable charging temperature against the actual cell.
100mA is a provisional conservative setting, not cell-specific approval.
The charger monitors its die temperature; no cell thermistor is implemented.

CONNECTORS
J3 OLED: 1 GND, 2 3V3, 3 SCL, 4 SDA. 2.54mm pitch.
J4 JST PH S2B-PH-K-S: 1 BAT_P, 2 BATT_NEG. 2.00mm pitch.
J5 debug: 1 SWCLK, 2 SWDIO, 3 GND, 4 3V3 reference, 5 UART_TX, 6 UART_RX.
J5 pin 4 is a target voltage reference, not an independent power input.
UART connections cross to the probe: target TX to probe RX and vice versa.
J6 load-cell pads: 1 E+ (3V3), 2 E- (GND), 3 A+, 4 A-.
Wire colours depend on the load cell; identify its wires from its specification.

FOOTPRINTS / MECHANICS
Pico hybrid footprint includes official castellated hand-solder pads and
1.0mm holes on the correct 17.78mm row spacing for removable sockets.
The three debug contacts are not in the 40-pin socket rows: socket-mounted
Pico requires SWD jumper connections from the module debug pads to the carrier.
The Pico antenna is toward the upper board edge. Keep its RF area free of
copper, metal enclosure material, battery foil and nearby wiring.
OLED module enclosure/standoff clearance must be reviewed in final assembly;
the PCB header footprint alone does not define the display's mounting height.

Buzzer land pattern follows Same Sky CPT-1203-03-SMT-TR drawing 2025-08-04:
two 6x3mm pads on 12mm centres. External PWM drive is required (~4kHz).
The local buzzer footprint does not include a manufacturer 3D body model.

USB4105 ground pads retain their centre positions and 0.6x1.15mm envelope;
corner radius is increased to 0.30mm. Its scoped NPTH clearance is 0.20mm,
matching JLCPCB's published NPTH-to-copper minimum. General board clearance
constraints remain unchanged. Review the local land-pattern modification
before manufacturing.
